    About this planner

    Built for students who live in a campus hostel and have a class, lab or exam in another school. Golf carts circle the campus, but at 8 am, at lunch and at 5 pm everyone needs one at once. The question is always the same: which cart do I need to catch, and will it get me there in time?

    Everything you see runs in your browser. The stops, routes, carts and timetables are sample records stored in the page’s JavaScript, and nothing you type leaves your device. Timetables are worked out from each route’s lap time and the number of carts on it, with extra carts at peak times. A later version will load these records from a server so they can be updated without changing the page.

    Sample data created for CS344 Web Engineering, Assignment 1. Stop positions follow the NUST campus map; routes, cart numbers and times are invented. Not an official NUST service.
